<a name="music"></a>

### 🎛️ Music tools

Browser instruments and tools for making music.

- **[mpump ↗](https://mpump.live)** — Drum machine and synth where the beat lives in the link. No install, no account. · [Source](https://github.com/gdamdam/mpump)
- **[mdrone ↗](https://mdrone.org)** — Microtonal drone instrument. Hold a tone, let the room evolve. · [Source](https://github.com/gdamdam/mdrone)
- **[mloop ↗](https://mloop.mpump.live/)** — Loop station and sampler. Record, loop, sample, perform. · [Source](https://github.com/gdamdam/mloop)
- **[padwright](https://github.com/gdamdam/padwright)** — Bank builder for the Roland SP-404MKII.

<a name="radio"></a>

### 📡 Radio experiments

From raw signals to something you can see.

- **[Meteor field reports ↗](https://gdamdam.github.io/meteor-satellite-reception/)** — Weather images captured with a garden antenna and SDR.
- **[nextpass](https://github.com/gdamdam/nextpass)** — Plan satellite passes from the terminal: weather satellites, space stations and ham birds.
- **[iqscan](https://github.com/gdamdam/iqscan)** — Find activity in SDR recordings and explore it in an offline report.
- **[KALLISTI](https://github.com/gdamdam/kallisti-meshcore-wio-l1pro)** — MeshCore firmware for the Wio Tracker L1 Pro. Read, reply and share your position without a phone.
- **[Meshgarden](https://github.com/gdamdam/meshgarden)** — A tiny LoRa BBS and network observatory for MeshCore and Meshtastic.
